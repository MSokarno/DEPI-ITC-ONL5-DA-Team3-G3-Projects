LIMITATIONS AND LESSONS
- The draft conflicts on source scope: it says 8 of 9 source tables were used, but its inventory marks four as used and the final database has four target tables. Do not claim eight source tables were used unless the team verifies the lineage.
- Some location charts may label cities as states.
- Tiny municipalities or categories with very few orders can produce misleading delivery averages or late rates. Explain this and recommend a minimum sample threshold.
- Delivery after the estimated date does not prove whether the cause is carrier performance or inaccurate estimates.
- Business recommendations are hypotheses to investigate, not causal conclusions proven by descriptive analysis.
- The dataset does not provide real names for every customer or product; identifiers and available attributes are used.
